Who pays?

  • Personal use is free: independent hobby projects and study, not work for an employer or an unlimited paid-business exemption.
  • Small groups are free: revenue plus funding (R) at most $500,000 and group people (H) at most 20; both must hold. Freelancers and solo companies use the same business test.
  • Larger groups pay the higher band: evaluate R and H separately, then choose whichever gives the higher fee.
  • Education and public benefit: qualifying teaching, research, charity and public-service purposes remain free regardless of core budget, funding or headcount. Commercial operations have specific rules (see the FAQ below and the license).
  • Try it: one group-wide evaluation of less than 32 consecutive calendar days, with productive work only as part of evaluating. No restarts or onward-hosting rights.

FAQ

I'm a freelancer.
Apply the same R/H rules, including funding. Being solo does not waive the revenue limit. Your own properly licensed tools may produce client deliverables without a client/output royalty under the Work for Clients conditions.
Only one person uses it at work.
Assess the entire employer group, not that person or department, even on a personal laptop or for unattended CI.
We added a 21st worker.
If counted at month end, that can start the free group's 90-day growth transition. Paid ordinary organic growth waits until renewal. A contractor counts from worked day 31 only while engaged.
Our client directs the bot.
Use the client's own permission or group license. A provider may buy/administer on its behalf. Selling provider-controlled hosted access needs the separate hosting agreement.
We are a school or public body.
Core qualifying purposes remain free regardless of donations, grants or investment funding. An exempt institution's commercial operation using Charness pays when its external commercial revenue exceeds $500,000, then using that operation's R/H. Headcount or funding alone does not trigger that institutional exception's commercial fee. Separate for-profit spin-offs and government trading companies follow ordinary rules from the start. Recognized for-profit schools have free teaching/learning, not commercial administration.
We already use MIT Charness.
MIT rights remain unchanged. Pre-existing MIT organizational adopters get one 90-day internal-use transition from first use of a newly licensed release, concurrent with evaluation, not a stackable extra trial.
We stop renewing.
Paid operation ends unless you independently qualify free or use an MIT release. You retain outputs and may keep archival copies, not a perpetual operating right.
Can we get a discount?
20% for a “Powered by Charness” mark in your product or on your site, 15% for prepaying 2 or 3 years; together 32% off. A prepaid term still moves up a band at an anniversary if you grow.
Refunds?
Full refund within 30 days after any payment, including renewal or a multi-year prepayment, no questions asked (once per group in any 12 months). See Refunds.
