Privacy

Sergei Solo handles licensing enquiries at charnesshub@gmail.com. Ordinary free use needs no registration. The licensing model requires no product compliance telemetry, activation or remote shutdown.

What we handle

For enquiries and orders we handle contact details, beneficiary entity and group scope, R/H bands, assessment date, attestations, discounts and where a discount mark is displayed, accepted terms, invoices, term dates and correspondence. We may also handle relevant eligibility-verification correspondence and accepted partner referral attribution. We do not routinely collect full accounts or named worker lists. You keep the underlying eligibility records for three years; verification accepts sufficient redacted accountant confirmations and never requires repositories, prompts, model credentials, customer data or production-system access.

What we use it for

We use these data for license administration, renewal, support of enquiries, proportionate compliance checks, refunds, partner settlement and legal/tax obligations. Access is limited to those purposes and authorized recipients; independent verification professionals must maintain confidentiality. We retain licensing and verification records only as needed for those purposes and applicable legal retention or disputes, then delete or anonymize them. Contact us for access, correction or deletion requests, subject to necessary legal retention.

This website

There is no online checkout. The site sets no analytics or non-essential cookies, and referrals use an introduction email or order code rather than tracking cookies.
