Refunds

Which payments can be refunded is set by LICENSE, section 3.8. This page only says how to ask.

  1. Write to charnesshub@gmail.com, preferably from the e-mail address your invoice was sent to.
  2. Give the invoice number. No reason is needed.
  3. We confirm by e-mail and return the money the way it was paid. If a merchant of record handled your order, we arrange the refund through it.

Mandatory legal rights are not affected.
